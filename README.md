🚀 Completed my Data Science & Analytics Task 3 with Future Interns — Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis!

📌 The problem:
I analyzed 41,000+ client records from a real bank marketing campaign to understand how leads move through the funnel — from being contacted → engaging → converting into customers — and where the biggest drop-offs happen.

🔍 Key insights I found:
✅ ~50% of leads never make it past a short call — the biggest drop-off in the whole funnel
✅ Cellular outreach converts nearly 3x better than landline (14.7% vs 5.2%)
✅ Smaller, targeted campaigns outperform mass outreach — low-volume months converted at 44-51% vs just 6-10% in high-volume months
✅ Retargeting past successful customers is the single highest-value segment — 65% conversion rate!
✅ Students and retirees convert far above average, opening the door for smarter audience segmentation

🛠️ Tools used: Python (pandas, matplotlib), Jupyter Notebook

This task really showed me how funnel analysis directly connects to revenue — it's not just about building charts, it's about finding the specific levers a business can pull to grow.
